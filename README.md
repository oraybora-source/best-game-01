# LENTERA LAUT — dive by lantern-light, bring the pearls home

A make.fun [best-game-01](https://make.fun/bounty/best-game-01) entry. A 2D canvas game,
plain static files, no network, no library.

Dive a glowing reef with a lantern. Collect pearls, mind your oxygen and your lamp
battery, and surface to bank your haul before the sharks find your light. Your lamp
draws treasure — and predators. Bank 500 pearls to become Master of the Reef.

- **Swim** with WASD or arrow keys (or hold the mouse), Shift to sprint.
- **Lamp**: press F to douse it — sharks lose your trail, but so do the pearls.
- **Bank**: surface with pearls to bank them. Spend the balance on Tank, Lamp and
  Fins upgrades between dives.
- **The Abyss**: the biggest pearls are down deep, guarded by the Warden, an
  anglerfish whose lure glows just like a pearl.

Run it: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080. Screenshots are staged
with `?shot=start|play|deep|end&seed=N`.
